Why the heart

Do you feel tense, overwhelmed, or worn out without quite knowing why? Have you already tried "breathing exercises" without ever knowing if they actually worked?

Heart coherence starts from a simple principle: your heart rate constantly varies, and that variability (HRV) reflects the state of your autonomic nervous system. By breathing at a precise rate — your own, measured individually — you can shift that system toward a physiological state of calm. With a biofeedback device, you watch that state form in real time, session after session.

This is not therapy and not a medical treatment. It's a concrete, measurable practice you take with you.

The method

01

Your resonance frequency

From the first session, we identify your optimal breathing rate — typically between 4.5 and 7 breaths per minute. That's the starting point of the whole programme.

02

Immediate visual feedback

The biofeedback device (emWave Pro) shows your coherence state in real time. You don't have to "believe" it's working — you see it.

03

An evidence-based method

Heart coherence with biofeedback is the most studied form of biofeedback for stress and wellbeing, with effectiveness comparable to mindfulness meditation on perceived stress.

This programme is for adults going through a period of prolonged stress or exhaustion, who are looking for a concrete tool rather than yet another general relaxation technique.

This is not medical or psychological care. If you're already being treated for burnout or another health issue, this programme is a complement to — never a replacement for — your medical care.
